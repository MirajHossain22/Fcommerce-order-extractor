## Lead Staff Frontend Engineer & UI/UX Design System Rules

Always enforce the design system and execution protocol outlined in `instructions.md`:

1. **Hallmark (Hierarchy Logic):** Force visual hierarchy primarily through element size, structural boldness, and precise placement over decorative clutter.
2. **Impeccable (Typography Rules):** Strict legibility, clear H1/H2 font scales (`Host Grotesk`), tabular numbers for money/order refs (`Atkinson Hyperlegible Mono`), no illegible decorative fonts.
3. **UI-UX Pro-Max (Conversion Optimization & WCAG):**
   - 60-30-10 Color Math: 60% Canvas (`--color-paper`), 30% Structural Brand (`--color-rule`, `--color-muted`), 10% Warm Amber Accent (`--color-accent`).
   - Frictionless mobile CTAs: minimum 44x44px touch targets.
   - High contrast ratios meeting WCAG AAA/AA on all interactive and hover states.
